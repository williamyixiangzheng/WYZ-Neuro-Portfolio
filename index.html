Y. William Zheng

Hi there, welcome to my personal webpage and computational neuroscience portfolio!

I'm an undergrad at Cornell University, Department of Neurobiology and Behavior (NBB). I'm interested in computational and cognitive neuroscience in studying learning, memory, spatial navigation, speech, and language. Particularly, I study hippocampal–prefrontal population dynamics and speech production at the single-cell level.

Research Publications Google Scholar GitHub Email

Research interests

I'm interested in how neural systems learn from experience, organize knowledge across time, generate speech and language, and use internal representations to guide flexible behavior. My work combines electrophysiology, statistical modeling, and neural population analyses.

Projects & Papers

Circuit mechanisms for disentangled representations and generalization in the hippocampus

Cornell University · Nature Neuroscience (2026)

Question: How does the hippocampus form neural representations that support rapid generalization across related experiences?

My contribution: Contributed to behavioral experiments, optogenetic surgeries, neural data preprocessing, and population-level analyses, supporting the investigation of hippocampal representation underlying rapid generalization.

A socio-spatial cognitive map of a naturalistic environment

Cornell University · manuscript in prep.

Question: How do hippocampal neural representations support spatial navigation and social interactions in naturalistic environments?

My contribution: Performed Neuropixels surgeries, support naturalistic multi-animal experiments, develop automated multi-animal identity-tracking workflows, and contribute to neural and behavioral data analyses.

Project page

Long-term, large-scale neural recordings across memory circuits with high-density flexible probes

Cornell University · manuscript in prep.

Question: How do hippocampal neural representations evolve over months of experience, and how reliably can individual neurons be tracked across long-term recordings(~300 days)?

My contribution: Conducted behavioral experiments and performed longitudinal analysis of single-neuron and place-cell stability across long-term recordings.

Temporal and laminar organization of linguistic representations during human sentence construction

Massachusetts General Hospital & Harvard University · manuscript in prep.

Question: How are linguistic and behavioral variables represented across cortical layers, time, and neural populations during human speech production?

More details will be updated once the manuscript is ready for public release. Feel free to reach out to me if you're interested in knowing more about this project!

Topological evolution of hippocampal–prefrontal manifolds during long-term learning

Cornell University · Honors thesis · ongoing

Question: How do hippocampal and prefrontal population representations change as animals learn a progressively expanded environment over extended timescales?

My contribution: Design longitudinal CA1-PFC population recordings; apply neural manifold, topological data analysis, and representational alignment methods to characterize how neural representations evolve during learning.

Cognitive Map Control Project

Cornell University · ongoing

Question: How do animals use memories that evolve over time to choose the most beneficial action? We set up automated Restaurant Row tasks with biased reward values to address this question.

My contribution: Contribute to animal behavioral tasks, experimental design and apparatus construction, brain-slice imaging, and preprocessing pipelines for spike sorting and DeepLabCut.

Muscle atrophy and gene-therapy research in Huntington's disease

Peking University

Question: Can CRISPR-based gene editing safely and durably reverse Huntington's disease pathology and behavioral deficits across different stages of disease progression?

My contribution (non-author contribution): Performed PCR, cell culture, mouse behavioral experiments, stereotaxic AAV injections, perfusion, and histological imaging.
