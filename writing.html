Proposals written as coursework or as preliminary concepts.

Closed-loop prioritized hippocampal replay in a brain-computer interface of value-biased choice

Course proposal (BioNB 3500 NeuroAI), Cornell University

A proposed closed-loop experiment asking whether hippocampal sequences can be biased in real time toward high-value trajectories, by analogy with prioritized experience replay in reinforcement learning, and whether such a bias would change how quickly an animal learns a probabilistic T-maze task. Written as a mini-grant proposal under the supervision of Dr. W. Sun.

  • Sharp-wave ripples
  • Hippocampal replay
  • Closed-loop optogenetics
  • Reinforcement learning
  • Brain–computer interface

Circuit mechanisms of representational drift and neural manifold change in Alzheimer's disease

Concept proposal, preliminary idea

A preliminary proposal asking whether the geometry of hippocampal population representations is altered in a mouse model of Alzheimer's disease, and whether such changes would be better explained by degraded upstream input or by disrupted local inhibitory dynamics. The proposed experiments have not been carried out.

  • Neural manifolds
  • Representational drift
  • Topological data analysis
  • Closed-loop optogenetics
  • Disease models
